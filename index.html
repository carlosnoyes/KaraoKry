How to play

  1. 1

    Pick a playlist you made. KaraoKry shuffles it and finds the lyrics for every song.

  2. 2

    Put on the headphones. You'll hear one song but see the words to a different one. Sing those words to the tune you hear.

  3. 3

    Everyone else guesses the song and the artist. When someone gets it, tap Got It! and pass the phone.

    If the headphones disconnect, the music may jump to the phone's speaker. Just tap pause.
